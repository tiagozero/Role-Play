Phrasal verbs

They light up as the story unfolds.

    Field reference

    Everything except lines is optional. Missing characters and verb definitions are generated automatically.

    title
    String. Shown on the card and in the player header.
    subtitle
    String. Short description under the title.
    scene
    String. Where and when the scene takes place.
    charactersoptional
    Array of objects. Each needs an id that matches the speaker values in lines. name, initials, side, color, soft, line and tagline are all optional. Unknown speakers get an auto-generated character.
    verbsoptional
    Object. Maps a phrasal verb to its definition. Keys are matched in lowercase. Any verb used in lines but missing here gets a placeholder definition.
    linesrequired
    Array of objects. Each line needs a speaker and a text (or choices, see below). The verb field is optional — if omitted, the app reads the *asterisks* in the text instead.
    lines[].idoptional
    String. A unique name so other lines or choices can jump here with next.
    lines[].choicesoptional
    Array of 2–4 objects. Turns the line into a choice point: the learner picks one reply (A–D) and the story follows it. The line needs no text; speaker is the character who replies. Each choice has text (with the *verb* highlighted), and optionally verb, next (the id to jump to) and feedback (a short note shown after picking).
    lines[].nextoptional
    String. The id to play after this line. Omit it to continue with the next line in the array; use "end" to stop. End every branch with next (to rejoin the story) or an ending, otherwise play runs on into the next branch.
    lines[].endingoptional
    String. Finishes the scene after this line and shows the title on the final banner. "end": true also finishes the scene, without a title.

    Example structure

    A branching role-play — one choice point with four paths and four endings. Linear scripts without choices still work exactly as before.
